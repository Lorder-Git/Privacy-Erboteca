Privacy policy for Erboteca

Last updated: November 25, 2026  ·  Version: 2.0

This is a translation. The reference text is the Italian version: if the versions differ, the Italian version prevails.

In short. Erboteca works on your phone. The data you enter stays there, except when you yourself choose a feature that sends it (you will find them in section 3): we do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising. You do not need an account, and the service is protected: encrypted connections, no advertising, no tracking. We protect your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and we are happy to help you: write to us whenever you like.

1. Who the data controller is

Hubert Montero, independent developer — marketplace-apps.com
Email: premium@marketplace-apps.com

2. Where your data stays

The data you enter in Erboteca (favorites, personal notes, pantry and settings) is saved only on your phone, in the storage reserved for the app.

3. When the app uses the internet

The app connects to the internet only for these features:

  • Updates: the app checks whether there is a new version and, if there is, downloads it from the website; it does not send the data you have entered or your email address. As with any internet connection, the providers see the phone's IP address; we do not save it.
  • Purchasing Premium: payment takes place on PayPal. We receive your email address and the order details to send you the key and for tax obligations.
  • Activating Premium: the app sends the key (which contains the email address used for the purchase) and a phone code created by the app (it does not contain your name or your number), to check that the key is valid and used on the permitted devices. Never the data you have entered in the app.
  • Content: the app downloads the entries, the photos and the plans with prices that it shows, in the free version too; Premium content only after activation. It does not send the data you have entered or the entries you look at. As with any internet connection, the providers see the phone's IP address; we do not save it.
  • External links: if you open another app or an external website, that service's privacy policy applies.

4. What Erboteca does not do

  • It has no accounts or registration.
  • It does not show advertising and does not carry out profiling.
  • It does not use third-party tracking or analytics tools.
  • It does not sell or pass on your data to anyone.
  • It does not read contacts, messages, location or other apps.
  • It does not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects concerning you or that similarly affect you (Art. 22 GDPR).

6. How long

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Record of the order, to send you the key: 90 days.
  • Sales data: it remains in the seller's PayPal account for 10 years, as required by the rules on keeping tax records (Art. 2220 of the Italian Civil Code).
  • Activations: as long as the license is in use; deleted within 90 days of the last use.
  • IP address: we do not save it; the providers keep it for the short time set by their services.
  • Messages and requests: if you have made a purchase, we keep them for 1 year (monthly license), 3 years (annual) or 5 years (three-year) from the last message, and in any case for no longer than 26 months after the license expires, if that date comes first: this is the period in which a dispute about the license could arise. If you have not made a purchase, 12 months from the last message, to follow up on any later requests. If a dispute is ongoing, we keep them until it is concluded. Requests about your rights and our replies follow the same periods.

7. Who receives the data

We do not give your data to anyone for their own purposes.

  • IT service providers that process the data on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR): Cloudflare (United States), Vercel (United States), Supabase (company based in Singapore; data stored in the European Union), Resend (United States).
  • Independent controllers, each with its own privacy policy: PayPal.

Transfers outside the European Union: Cloudflare, Vercel and Resend participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, recognized as adequate by the European Commission; for Supabase, the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission apply. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards at premium@marketplace-apps.com.

8. Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its use, object, and receive it in a readable format.

  • For the data on your phone, you do this directly in the app: with Premium active, by deleting notes and pantry in Altro › Note personali (More › Personal notes) and Altro › Dispensa (More › Pantry), and favorites with “Togli dai preferiti” (Remove from favorites) in the entry, or by uninstalling the app.
  • For the little data we receive, write to us at premium@marketplace-apps.com: we will reply within one month. To help us find it faster, you can give us your license key or write to us from the email address you used for the purchase.
  • You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time, without affecting what was done before.
  • If you believe that your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it).

Right to object. When we process data for our legitimate interest (protecting licenses, keeping the service secure), you can object whenever you like by writing to premium@marketplace-apps.com. We will stop, unless there are overriding legitimate grounds, which we will explain to you.

9. Security

The data on your phone is kept in the storage reserved for the app, protected by the operating system. Communications with our services are encrypted. We recommend keeping your screen lock on and your phone up to date.

10. Minors

Erboteca is intended for adults (18 years of age or older) and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Disclaimer

Erboteca is a reference tool and is not medical advice. Before using a plant or a recipe, ask a doctor or a pharmacist.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with its date.
