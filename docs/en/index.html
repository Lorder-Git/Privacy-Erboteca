Privacy policy — Erboteca

Last updated: November 24, 2026  ·  Version: 1.2

This is a translation. The reference text is the Italian version: if the versions differ, the Italian version prevails.
In short.
  • Erboteca does not collect data about your health. It does not ask about your symptoms, illnesses, medicines, weight or age, and it does not build a profile of you (section 3).
  • Your favorites, notes and pantry stay only on your phone. We do not send them to anyone, we cannot see them and, if you lose them, we cannot recover them.
  • There is no sign-up and no account. No advertising, no artificial intelligence, no usage statistics about you. We only keep anonymous daily counts, with no identifiers (section 7).
  • The app connects to the internet only to activate Premium, to check for new plant pages and download the ones reserved for Premium, and for updates (sections 5, 6 and 7). In the free version too, the app checks for new plant pages, to show them to you with a padlock, and downloads the updated list of poisonous plants: these connections contain no data about you and no license. These connections contain nothing of what you read, save or write in the app.
  • You pay for Premium only once, on marketplace-apps.com, through PayPal: no subscription and no automatic renewal (section 8).
  • Erboteca offers information and traditional recipes. It is not a medical service and does not replace a doctor or pharmacist (section 15).

3. This app does not collect data about your health

Erboteca is a library of herbs: plant pages, homemade recipes, tips on preparing and storing them, and guidance on when it is best to avoid them. To use it, we do not ask you anything about your health.

  • The app has no health profile: it does not ask about your symptoms, illnesses, medicines you take, allergies, pregnancy, weight or age.
  • The app does not suggest remedies based on what is happening to you and does not ask you about your symptoms.
  • The warnings (for example "not during pregnancy" or "not if you take certain medicines") are the same for everyone: you read them and you decide. The app does not know whether they apply to you.
  • When you first open the app, we ask you to confirm that you are of legal age and that you have read the warnings. This confirmation stays only on your phone, is not sent to us and does not contain your date of birth.

In your notes you can write whatever you like. If you decide to write something about your health there, that text stays only on your phone like everything else: we do not receive it and cannot read it. The same goes for favorites: even if the list of plants you save could suggest something about you, it does not leave the phone.

4. Favorites, notes and pantry

In the app you can save, if you wish:

  • favorites: the plants and recipes you want to find again;
  • notes: your own free texts about a plant or a recipe;
  • pantry: the herbs and ingredients you have at home, with the information you choose to add;
  • settings: language, app appearance, and confirmation of the warnings shown the first time you open the app.

Everything stays only in the app's private storage on your phone, which Android keeps separate from other apps. It is not sent to anyone: we do not receive it. You can change or delete these data at any time in the app; uninstalling the app deletes them all.

The basic plants and recipes are already in the app and can be viewed offline too.

5. Activating Premium on your phone

A Premium key works on up to 2 phones. To enforce this limit, when you activate the key, the app connects to our license server (a service on Cloudflare that we manage) and sends:

  • the name of the app;
  • the license key, which contains the buyer's email address, the plan and the expiry date;
  • a phone fingerprint: a code derived with a hash function from the name of the app and the phone's Android ID, then shortened. The Android ID is never sent in clear, and the fingerprint is different for each app, so it cannot be used to link your use of different apps.

These requests do not contain your favorites, notes or pantry. The same request is repeated automatically when the activation is about to expire, and only if the phone is online; it is also made when you tap Release this phone. The server replies with a signed activation that the app checks on the phone, even offline.

The license server does not keep your email or the key in clear. It keeps only: the hash of the key; the phone fingerprint; the type of plan; the date of activation and the date of the last renewal; if you change phones, the date of the last automatic replacement (one replacement is allowed every 30 days); if a key is blocked (for example after a refund or abuse), the hash of that key. The Release this phone button, on the Premium screen, deletes from the server the record that links the key to that phone and frees up the slot.

The Android ID is read only to activate Premium, never for advertising, statistics or profiling.

6. New plants reserved for Premium

In addition to the plants already in the app, over time we publish pages for new plants, with their photos. With a paid plan, the app downloads the new pages from our service, checking the license.

  • For the check, the app sends this phone's activation: the signed code that the license server gave it when you activated Premium (section 5), not the license key. We do not keep it after the check.
  • We do not record which plants you read or save.
  • Your favorites, notes and pantry are not sent.

Downloaded pages stay on the phone and can be viewed offline too.

When it starts, the app asks our service for the list of new pages (only titles and plant names), even in the free version, to show them with a padlock. The request contains no personal data and no license data.

Also in the free version, the app downloads the updated list of poisonous plants from our service, as safety information for everyone. This request also contains no personal data and no license data, and we do not record which pages you read. Downloaded pages stay on the phone and can be viewed offline too.

7. App updates and statistics

When it starts, the app checks whether there is a new version and, if there is, downloads it from our service. You confirm the installation: Android always asks you. The check contains no personal data and no license data.

To know how much the app is used, we keep only anonymous daily counts, with no identifiers: they cannot be traced back to anyone and are not personal data.

As with any internet connection, the providers that host our services (section 10) see the IP address, the type of device and the time of the request, and keep them in their technical logs for the period set by their services.

The plant pages are designed for plants that are common in Italy, and the rules on picking plants in the wild may vary from place to place.

13. Security

  • Favorites, notes and pantry are in the app's private storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps.
  • Communications with the license server, for new plants and for updates use HTTPS.
  • On the license server we keep only hashes instead of the key and the phone ID (section 5); the activation is signed and the app checks it on the phone.
  • The license key contains your email: do not share it with other people.

We recommend keeping your phone's screen lock on and updating the operating system. Since we do not keep copies of your data, if you lose or reset your phone, or uninstall the app, your favorites, notes and pantry cannot be recovered.

14. Minors

The app is intended for adults. The recipes to drink are meant for adults only: they are not suitable for children and teenagers, or during pregnancy or breastfeeding. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

15. The app is not a medical service

Erboteca collects traditional information and recipes about common plants. It does not diagnose, does not give personal medical advice, does not replace a doctor, a pharmacist or an ongoing treatment, and does not promise cures.

The pages are general information gathered from public sources and have not been checked case by case by a doctor or pharmacist.

Recognizing a plant in the wild can be difficult, and some dangerous plants look like the ones used in the recipes. If you have even the slightest doubt, do not pick it and do not use it.

If you take medicines, are pregnant or are breastfeeding, ask a doctor or pharmacist first. If you feel unwell, call 112 or 118 (the emergency numbers in Italy), or contact a Poison Control Center (Centro Antiveleni).
